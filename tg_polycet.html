Preparation Strategy

Important Tips to Score High

Follow these strategies to maximise your TG Polycet score in the least time.

1

Master SSC Concepts First

Polycet is based entirely on Class 10 syllabus. Ensure all SSC formulas and concepts are clear before attempting advanced problems.

2

No Negative Marking — Attempt All

Since there's no negative marking, attempt every single question. Even guessing gives you a 25% chance per MCQ. Never leave blanks.

3

Focus on Maths for Rank Boost

Maths is the scoring differentiator. Students who score 18–20 in Maths usually land top ranks. Practice daily calculations.

4

Solve Previous Year Papers

The last 10 years' papers repeat concepts heavily. Identify recurring topics and practice those chapters thoroughly.

5

Time Management in the Hall

With 60 questions in 2 hours, you have 2 minutes per question. Skip difficult ones, finish easy ones first, then return.

6

Weekly Mock Tests Are Key

Take at least one full mock test every week. Review mistakes, track weak chapters, and improve before the exam day.

7

Chemistry: Memorise Equations

Chemical reactions and equations are high-yield in TG Polycet. Write them daily until they're second nature.

8

Physics: Diagrams & Derivations

Understand diagrams conceptually — optics and electricity diagrams appear regularly. Don't skip numerical derivations.

9

Revise 30 Days Before Exam

The final month should be 100% revision + mock tests. No new topics. Strengthen weakest areas with targeted practice.
